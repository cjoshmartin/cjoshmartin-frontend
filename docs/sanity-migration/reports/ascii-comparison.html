Rejected first approach: CDN scales to the grid

Same renderer, but the CDN resizes straight to the grid (?w=35&h=15&fit=scale&fm=png) and the floor is applied afterwards. The CDN's resampler is not Pillow's bicubic filter (closest to Pillow's Lanczos on the 800 px photos), so on the smaller photos a third of the characters move by more than one step.
